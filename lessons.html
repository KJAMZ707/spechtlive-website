Equipment policy

All production gear and software is provided by Kieran by default. If you'd rather use your own equipment or software, that's no problem. The hourly rate is a flat floor — it doesn't change based on subject, and only increases if a rental or insurance arrangement on provided equipment is required. Instructor-owned instruments can also be rented for an additional fee — use the rental request below for details.
